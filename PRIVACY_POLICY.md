# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2025-01-01

This Privacy Policy describes how DRM Info for Android ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with your use of our application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Collected Automatically
When you use the App, we may collect certain information automatically through third-party services:

*   **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data (such as advertising IDs, IP addresses, and interaction data) to provide personalized ads. For more information, please review how Google uses data when you use their partners' sites or apps: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
*   **Firebase Analytics:** The App may use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage data (e.g., session duration, device models, and app crashes) to help us improve the App. This data does not personally identify you.

### B. Information You Provide
The App inspects and displays hardware and DRM-related specifications of your device. This information is processed locally on your device. We do not transmit or store this data on our servers. If you choose to use the "Share Report" feature, the data is generated locally and shared via the apps you select (e.g., email or messaging apps).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected automatically (via Google services) for the following purposes:
*   To serve advertisements and measure their performance.
*   To analyze app usage and improve performance and stability.

## 3. Data Safety and Permissions

The App requires specific Android permissions to function properly (e.g., Internet access to serve ads). It does not request invasive permissions (such as contacts, location, or microphone) and only accesses hardware APIs to read DRM capabilities.

## 4. Consent and GDPR/CCPA Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or California, you will be presented with a consent form (via the Google User Messaging Platform) upon launching the App. This allows you to manage your preferences regarding personalized advertising and data collection. You can revoke or change your consent at any time.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue on the GitHub repository: [https://github.com/seventhmoon/DrmInfo-Android/issues](https://github.com/seventhmoon/DrmInfo-Android/issues).
